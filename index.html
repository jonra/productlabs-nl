05 / FAQ

Working with Claude through ProductLabs

What is Anthropic's Claude and why should my business care?

Claude is Anthropic's frontier AI assistant, one of the most capable large language models available. It excels at reasoning, analysis, coding and natural language tasks. For businesses, Claude can automate knowledge work, power intelligent product features and handle complex workflows that previously required human judgment. ProductLabs helps you identify where Claude creates the most value and builds the integrations to make it happen.

What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

MCP is an open standard developed by Anthropic that lets Claude connect to external tools, databases and services. MCP servers act as bridges between Claude and your business systems. They allow Claude to read your data, take actions in your tools and automate end to end workflows. ProductLabs builds custom MCP servers tailored to your tech stack and business needs.

What are Claude skills and plugins?

Claude skills are specialized instruction packages that give Claude domain specific capabilities, such as generating legal documents, analyzing financial data or following your brand guidelines. Plugins bundle multiple skills, MCP servers and tools into installable packages. ProductLabs designs and builds custom skills and plugins that make Claude an expert in your specific workflows and ready to deploy across your team.

Do I need a large team or budget to use Claude?

No. Anthropic's Claude API is accessible and pay per use, which makes it suitable for Small-Medium businesses.

How long does a typical project take?

Claude implementation timelines depend on scope. Most projects start at around 4 weeks and extend upward based on the number of workflows and integrations.

Do you only work with Claude?

Yes. We specialise in Anthropic’s Claude ecosystem. This focus allows us to deliver deeper, cleaner and more reliable implementations.

Do you build the solution or does our team?

Your team builds the solution so ownership and knowledge stay with you. We guide architecture, development and deployment to ensure a smooth implementation.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We integrate Claude through MCP servers, APIs and custom agents so it works with your current tools, data and processes.

Do you offer ongoing support?

We support adoption through training, documentation and governance. After implementation, you can continue working independently or request periodic optimisation.
